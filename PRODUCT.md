## Brand Commitments
Snowlight Studio uses an original voxel developer and timber workstation, a transparent-portrait headshot, neutral dark/ivory themes, and a warm accent. The character greets visitors once per page load, reveals the portfolio, returns to work, and cycles through typing, frustration, and short walks. The scene supports unrestricted trackball rotation and zoom; its lamp is permanently on. The header retains the original monogram, music settings, and theme toggle. Typography is exclusively locally hosted DM Sans.

The six content sections appear one at a time. Reading is proportional within each section; crossing a boundary adds progressive mechanical resistance, synthesized ticks/clacks, and optional haptics. Music uses the user-supplied local recordings and remains independent of scrolling effects. Browser audio restrictions apply. Reduced motion skips the welcome and wandering cycle and disables scroll compliance; hidden tabs pause rendering. craftz.dog is a visual reference, not an asset or code source. Earlier review documents remain historical. Current behavior is described in README.md and docs/tactile-scrolling.md.
